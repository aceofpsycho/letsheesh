What's in the cup

Five layers, built from the bottom up. Each one earns its place.

  1. Leche flan

    Silky custard steamed separately, laid as the creamy bedrock. Sweet, smooth, and unmistakable.

    the heart

  2. Sliced mangoes

    Sweet Philippine carabao mangoes, sliced fresh. The reason it tastes like summer even in December.

    the flavor

  3. Cream

    Chilled all-purpose cream whipped light. Cold, airy, and the first thing you feel on a hot day.

    the cool

  4. Crushed graham

    Crunchy base layer that gives the cup its structure and sweet crunch.

    the base

  5. Your drizzle

    Chocolate or melted sugar for the perfect finishing touch.

    the finish

About Us

Our Product

Our product "Mango Leche Flan" represents our delicious and refreshing dessert. Our logo features a picture of the dessert at the center. It highlights its unique layers and sweet flavor. The blue comic-style background represents excitement and creativity. The water splashes symbolize freshness. The bright yellow, orange, and red colors make the logo eye-catching. They reflect the sweetness and deliciousness of our product. Overall, our logo represents a fun, refreshing, and enjoyable dessert we offer to our customers.

Vision

To make Mango Leche Flan the best-selling dessert product in our school. We want Mango Leche Flan to be the go-to dessert for students whenever they need a refreshing break or a sweet pick-me-up. Our goal is for our unique layered desserts to bring genuine smiles to our campus community with every single order.

Mission

To bring a refreshing and delicious dessert product that becomes the go-to dessert for students in our school. Through our layered dessert that contains leche flan, sliced mangoes, crushed graham, and cream, we aim to offer an enjoyable dessert to students that satisfies cravings. It promotes customer satisfaction and leaves customers with a refreshing feeling.
