# Surface brief: index.html

## Scope & mode

The product's single landing page. Mode: **Persuade** — the visitor decides and acts (orders a cup); design is the product.

## Audience, job, action

Primary: senior high school students on their phones, mid-morning break, in the heat — decide in seconds whether this is worth ₱30-35 and where to get it. Secondary: teachers as buyers; evaluators as pitch readers.

- Job: know what it is, see the layers, see the price, order.
- Action: order via message or walk-up (real channels); the checkout section is a labeled demo.
- Proof: the visible layers, the honest price, the demo checkout. No testimonials, no invented claims.

## Constraints

- Photo slots must be trivially replaceable by a non-developer (clearly marked, simple filenames, comment instructions).
- Mock checkout labeled as demo; must not fake real payment.
- Price ₱30 plain / ₱35 with both drizzles — always visible, never buried.
- Mobile-first (phones are the primary device).
- Name: **Mango Leche Flan**.

## Chosen direction

Canon — the category standard (modern drink-shop landing page) executed at full craft. Craft bar: milkshake/boba shop sites — playful but disciplined type, big product presence, punchy CTAs, appetizing color, mobile-first.

## Memorable moment

The layered cup rendered as an honest, labeled, delicious cross-section — the layers are the product's proof and the page's visual signature.

## Unresolved

- The user's real messaging handle (placeholder stays clearly marked).
- Real product photography (user will supply; slots reserved).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The one idea this surface owns is *the visible layers as proof* — a dessert page where the cross-section of the cup does the selling that adjectives would fake. It refuses the category-default arrangement of "stock dessert photo + generic hero copy + button": no fake photo, no unearned claims, the product's own structure is the evidence.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cream ground (#FFF8EC), mango-orange as the committed color (#F59E0B family carrying CTA band and prices), deep cocoa-brown ink (#3A2317), flan-gold and graham-tan as supporting layer colors. Component language: soft rounded cards (16-20px radius), chunky pill CTAs, sticker-like price chips, one consistent hand-drawn warmth — like a modern boba shop menu board.

STORY: The visitor understands in five seconds what the cup is (mango float + leche flan, two desserts, one cup), believes it because they can see the layers, knows exactly what it costs (₱30/₱35 never buried), and knows exactly how to get one (message or walk-up — stated plainly, demo checkout labeled).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone-first. Top bar: name wordmark left, "Order" anchor right. Below, stacked: one-line hook ("Two desserts. One cup. Built for the heat."), the layered-cup cross-section as the dominant visual (~40% of viewport), price chip pair (₱30 / ₱35) directly under the cup, one primary CTA ("Order for lunch break") full-width, secondary link ("See the layers ↓"). Desktop: hook + copy left, cup right, prices and CTA under the copy.

FORM: The category standard (canon), chosen on the decision page. Position: n/a (canon takes no list position). seed: none (pinned) — the user chose the canon card outright, quoting "the category standard".

Signature interaction: the cup's layers highlight in sequence on scroll/hover — flan → mango → cream → graham — with a matching layer label, turning the product diagram into an interactive menu.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
